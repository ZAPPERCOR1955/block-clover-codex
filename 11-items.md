# Items and Equipment

*Block Clover 1.8.1 (NeoForge 1.21.1). Read from the mod's code; numbers change between versions.*

Most Block Clover items are either **summoned by a spell** or **bought with Yul**. Very little drops from ordinary mobs. This page lists every player-facing item, what it does and where it comes from. Affix ranges for accessories and wands are in [Mana and the Anti-Magic Resource](04-mana-and-anti-magic.md).

## Anti-Magic swords

The four swords are the first four "spells" of Anti-Magic. Casting one pulls it out of the grimoire into your hand; if you already carry it, the cast recalls it. They never break, and each sword levels from 1 to 10 by landing hits (12 XP per hit). Holding one in either hand sets your Anti-Magic regeneration rate.

| Sword | Damage | Attack speed | Use (right click) | Shift + use | Regen while held | How to get |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demon-Slayer Sword | 9 | 0.8 | Reflect incoming spells for 1 s (5 s cooldown). Hold past 30% charge to throw. | Summon its rideable flying form (needs the Demon-Slayer Flight node) | 50% | Starter spell; 50 Anti-Magic to summon |
| Demon Dweller | 7 | 1.1 | Give Anti-Magic to an aimed ally for 60 s (5 min cooldown) or nearby squadmates (10 min). Hold to throw. | Copy a supported friendly magic (30 s cooldown); copied Black Slash gains ×1.5 damage and speed | 75% | The special reward in any dungeon's personal vault chest, guaranteed for Anti-Magic users; 100 to summon afterwards |
| Demon-Destroyer Sword | 8 | 0.8 | Cleanse harmful effects and glowing from a target or yourself (15 s). Hold to throw. | Erase magic within 5 blocks (15 s cooldown) | 100% | Discovered once Demon Dweller is known; 150 to summon |
| Demon Slasher Katana | 8 | 1.4 | Dante Slash, 40 base damage (1 min cooldown). Hold to throw. | Hold to throw; tap for the Selective Cutting menu | 50% | Discovered once Demon-Destroyer is known; 150 to summon |
| Black Divider | 15 at 1.5× | 0.6 | Grow by 0.5× | Shrink by 0.5× | 50% | The Black Divider technique turns Demon-Slayer into it for 60 s |

Black Divider needs an active Anti-Magic form and caps at 2× in Black Form, 3× in Berserk Mode and 5× in Devil Union. Larger sizes add damage and reach and lose attack speed. The Sword Recall node calls thrown swords back. A player **without** Anti-Magic who carries any of these loses mana every tick (1 for Demon-Slayer or Black Divider, 2 for Dweller or Slasher, 3 for Destroyer).

## Brooms

A **Broom** carries you and one passenger. Its speed is rolled once by rarity:

| Rarity | Speed |
|---|---|
| Common | 90% to 100% |
| Uncommon | 100% to 110% |
| Rare | 110% to 120% |
| Epic | 120% to 130% |
| Legendary | 130% to 140% |
| Mythic | 140% to 150% |

Sources: the **entrance exam** grants a permanent Common broom after the flying test (Anti-Magic users receive it without flying); **bandits** drop one with their own rarity on a high tool roll, once per bandit; the **Equipment Merchant** sells one for 1,600 Yul and the **Black Market Dealer** for 6,500; auctions list them by rarity. Witch race nodes add broom speed on top.

The **Crazy Cyclone** is Magna's broom: 1.5× speed and three riders. Magna offers rides on it, free for Black Bulls, and since 1.8.1 Faust House is a destination at double the normal fare. The item is not sold anywhere. Spell mounts such as Swift White Hawk, Heavenly Wind Ark and Sea Dragon's Cradle are spells, not items.

## Accessories

Rings, earrings, bracelets and necklaces, two of each, for eight slots. Six rarities, up to five affixes each; the ranges are in [Mana and the Anti-Magic Resource](04-mana-and-anti-magic.md).

| Source | What it offers |
|---|---|
| Bandits | On death, 60% of their tool roll gives an accessory of the bandit's rarity |
| Equipment Merchant | An Uncommon ring for 900 Yul |
| Black Market Dealer | Epic ring or bracelet 14,000, Rare necklace 7,500, and the full set by tier: Rare 7,500, Epic 14,000, Legendary 40,000, Mythic 120,000 |
| Black Market auction | Five listings every 30 minutes; base price 75, 150, 300, 650, 1,400 or 3,000 by rarity |
| Selling back | The dealer buys accessories at 100, 300, 2,000, 5,000, 14,000 and 45,000 Yul by rarity |

Buying a Mythic accessory from a dealer earns the "A Very Expensive Mistake" title.

## Wands

Any item tagged as a wand rolls a cast-time affix, a mana-output affix and sometimes a third support affix the first time it is held. Bandits drop them on a middle tool roll, and merchants list them at 1,800 and 8,500 Yul depending on stock. Wand bonuses apply only while held.

## Magic stones

Eleven colours: white, green, yellow, red, orange, cyan, blue, pink, magenta, lavender and grey. Each **distinct** colour in your inventory gives +200 max mana, +5% magic damage and +10% mana per regeneration pulse; a second stone of the same colour adds nothing. Sources: the Black Market Dealer sells one colour per visit for 30,000 Yul, dungeon stone chests hold one random colour, and every version of Yuno drops the blue stone 2% of the time. All eleven together are worth +2,200 max mana and +55% magic damage, and carrying all eleven at once earns the "Gotta Catch Them All" title.

## Magic crystals

Blue crystal clusters grow inside dungeons in small, medium and large sizes. Mining them gives **magic crystal shards**, which the Supply and Equipment Merchants buy at 100 Yul each (up to 128 per merchant); the Black Market Dealer does not take them.

## Form armour and cosmetics

- **Black Form Head and Wing, Berserk Form set, Asta Devil Union set, Yami Demon Form set, Valkyrie Dress armour, Slash Armor set, Spirit Dive crown and mantle** are the visuals of transformations. The game equips them for you while the form is active and removes them after; since 1.8.0 they cannot be taken off in survival. They are not loot.
- **Devil Arm** is the chest-slot item that represents Liebe's arm bargain.
- **Squad cloaks** come from **Vanessa** for 500 Yul, free for Black Bulls. She has a design for eight of the nine squads and says so when yours is missing. You must already be in the squad.
- **Vanessa's Hat and Magna's Glasses** are character headwear; no drop or shop gives them in 1.8.1.

## Black Market auction rules

- Five listings per auction, every 30 minutes by default (the `blackMarketResetMinutes` gamerule). Rarity odds per listing: 40% Common, 27% Uncommon, 17% Rare, 10% Epic, 5% Legendary, 1% Mythic.
- The first bid must meet the base price. After that the minimum raise is a tenth of the base price (at least 10), growing 12% per bid up to 30 steps and never more than the base price.
- A bid in the last 30 seconds resets the timer to 30 seconds. Outbid players are refunded at once, or on next login. In the final minute you cannot switch to a different listing, only rebid on the one you chose, and a withdrawn bid keeps the listing's minimum at the last price.
- Winning a Legendary or Mythic listing locks you out of Legendary and Mythic bids in the next auction.
- Accessories, wands and brooms are rolled with the listing's rarity when they are delivered.

## Luck and loot

Your luck percentage adds bonus loot rolls: half a roll per 100% luck, up to 12 extra rolls, on mob drops, fishing and ore blocks. Luck comes from the Luck mastery, race and accessory affixes and a four-leaf grimoire (+100%).
