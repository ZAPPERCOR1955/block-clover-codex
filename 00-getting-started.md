# Getting Started

*Block Clover 1.8.1 (NeoForge 1.21.1). Read from the mod's code; numbers change between versions.*

This is the first hour, in order. Every step links to the page with the full numbers.

## 1. Your origin

When you first join, the **Black Clover Origin Roll** screen opens. It rolls your race (Human with a class, Elf, Witch, Dwarf or Hybrid) and your magic from the fifteen playable origins. You get a number of **reroll tokens** set by the server, then press **Confirm**. If the server has the `randomMagic` gamerule off you can pick your magic directly instead. Anti-Magic always makes you a Human Peasant. Details: [Races and Classes](10-races-and-classes.md).

You also receive the guide book. Keep it; `/blockclover guide` gives it back.

## 2. Controls

Default keys. All of them can be changed in Minecraft's Controls menu.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Z | Use the selected spell. Hold to charge it for more output at extra mana cost. |
| R | Switch spell (opens the spell wheel). |
| G | Grimoire Menu: stats, masteries, skill trees, missions, quests, bonds, social, settings. |
| V | Use the selected skill (Mana Skin, Mana Zone, Mana Method, Ki, Zetten and so on). |
| C | Switch skill. |
| Shift + V | Alternate skill use: Mana Zone editor, cycle the Mana Method order, Mana Pressure from Mana Sense. |
| X | Switch mode for skills that have one (Zetten strike type). |
| H | Transformations wheel. |
| B | Dash (needs the Leg Strength Flash Step node). |
| J | Gallus Crow, for Shadow users in Devil Union. |

## 3. Get your grimoire

Find a **Grimoire Tower** and talk to **Drouot**. The grimoire descends over about seven seconds; you cannot cast until it arrives. There is a 2% chance it is a four-leaf grimoire, more with luck, which gives +100% luck for good. You receive one starter spell immediately. Completing this also finishes the first story quest and pays 250 XP, 500 Yul and 5 stat points. Details: [Magic Types and Spells](09-magics-and-spells.md).

## 4. Spend your first points

Open the grimoire menu (G). You start with 10 stat points and get 5 more per level. Points cost 1 each until a stat reaches 20, so spreading early points is cheap. Max Mana, Mana Regen Speed and Magic Damage pay off for every caster; Strength and the two defenses for melee builds. Details: [Stats and Masteries](03-stats-and-masteries.md).

## 5. Learn spells by using magic

Spells are discovered, not bought. Three things roll a discovery: casting a spell, taking a hit, and landing a melee hit. The chance climbs steeply the lower your health is and while you are fighting hostile mobs, and it is doubled while you know only one spell. Every cast also gives you 5 XP and levels the spell itself. The first few spells come fast; later ones come from real fights. Details: [Spell Discovery and Levels](06-spell-levels-and-discovery.md).

## 6. Missions and XP

Mob kills do not give XP on their own. XP comes from casting (Magic Bullet excepted), missions, quests, bandits and dungeons. Open the Missions tab, accept up to three Easy missions (cast 5 spells, kill 5 mobs, deliver supplies) and let them complete themselves. Claim story quests in the Quests tab as they turn Ready; see [Quests](12-quests.md). Ten Easy completions unlock Normal, ten Normal unlock Hard, each unlock paying 5 stat points. Details: [Leveling and Points](02-leveling-and-points.md) and [Missions and Economy](13-missions-and-economy.md).

## 7. The entrance exam and a squad

Find the **Entrance Exam arena** and register with the examiner. Exams run every 30 minutes; be back before the start. Four tests follow (broom, ability, control, combat). Score above 65 and at least one squad offers a place; each squad has its own bar, lowered by fitting magic, noble or royal blood, 100 reputation or 180 max mana.

Joining a squad matters more than it looks: **merit**, and therefore Magic Knight rank, only accumulates while you are in one, and squads pay Yul every hour.

## 8. Skill points and the Mana tree

Every fifth level gives a skill point. The Mana tree opens with Mana Sense, Reinforcement Magic and Mana Recovery. Mana Skin and Mana Zone are gated behind **Mereoleona's trials** at Yultim Volcano, which sits 6,500 to 7,200 blocks from spawn; bring a plan for the heat. **Ki** is separate: pay Yami 500 Yul, then get hit by projectiles until it awakens. Details: [Skill Trees](05-skill-trees.md).

## 9. Items and money

Yul comes from missions, quests, squad pay and selling to travelling merchants. The **Black Market** auction, five listings every 30 minutes, is the main source of accessories, and rings, earrings, bracelets and necklaces stack to eight slots. Each distinct **magic stone** you carry adds 200 max mana and 5% magic damage. Details: [Items and Equipment](11-items.md) and [Mana and Anti-Magic](04-mana-and-anti-magic.md).

## 10. If you rolled Anti-Magic

Your resource bar is Anti-Magic and only regenerates well with a sword in hand. Level each sword by hitting things with it (about 38 hits to level 4). Demon-Slayer is your starter; **Demon Dweller only comes from a dungeon's personal vault chest**, and Destroyer and Slasher are discovered after it, in that order. Your skill tree is the Anti-Magic tree, bought with the same skill points. Black Form and Berserk need no contract. Devil Union needs **Liebe**: reach level 25 with Black Form, accept his **Devil Arm** bargain when you are nearly dead, then put an Anti-Magic sword on the altar at **Faust House** and win the fight. Details: [Bonds and Liebe](08-bonds-and-liebe.md) and [Transformations](07-transformations.md). The whole path, level by level, is in [The Anti-Magic Route](01-anti-magic-route.md).

## 11. If you rolled Fire, Water or Wind

A spirit (Salamander, Undine or Sylph) can choose you once you reach the server's minimum level: a 1 in 1,000 roll every five minutes, or at once with a **spirit scroll** from a dungeon vault. The bond then grows from 0% to 100% by fighting with the matching magic: Spirit Dive at 25%, Full Spirit Dive at 50%, Saint Stage at 100%, with damage, mana and regen bonuses every 10%. Details: [Bonds, Spirits and Liebe](08-bonds-and-liebe.md).

## 12. Your first dungeon

Every hour of server time a stone **dungeon gate** appears in the Overworld within 2,500 blocks of the players, and your squad captain messages its coordinates; track the First Dungeon quest to see the marker. It closes after 15 minutes if nobody enters. Inside, help defeat **Mars** or reach the **treasure vault**, open your personal vault chest, and escape before the collapse. Each milestone pays XP, Yul and squad stars, and the chest holds accessories plus a special reward: Demon Dweller for Anti-Magic users, a chance at a spirit scroll for everyone else. Details: [World, Structures and NPCs](14-world-and-npcs.md).

## Common questions

- **Where is the config file?** There is none. Servers use gamerules and `/blockclover`; see the [Admin Reference](15-admin-reference.md).
- **Why can I not buy Mana Skin?** You are an Anti-Magic user; the Mana tree is replaced by the Anti-Magic tree.
- **Why am I not gaining merit?** Merit only counts while you are in a squad.
- **My mana drains for no reason.** You are carrying an Anti-Magic sword without being an Anti-Magic user. Every sword in your inventory drains mana every tick.
- **Creative mode stopped my progress.** Creative players cannot discover spells, earn bond experience, claim dungeon rewards or complete challenges.
- **Where is Demon Dweller?** In your personal vault chest inside any dungeon. It is no longer discovered by fighting.
